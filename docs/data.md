# Data

## What is and is not in the repository

| Item | In the repository? |
| --- | --- |
| Synthetic OHLCV series (`data/demo/synthetic_ohlcv.csv`) | Yes, generated by [`scripts/make_demo_ohlcv.py`](../scripts/make_demo_ohlcv.py) |
| VN30 and BID market files | **No.** `data/raw/` is ignored by Git |
| Derived tables and figures from the real files (`results/vn30/`, `results/bid/`) | Yes: aggregate metrics, intervals' summary statistics and plots; no raw price table |

Market data are not redistributed because the provider and its redistribution terms have not
been documented for these files. To reproduce the committed results, place the two files at
`data/raw/vn30.csv` and `data/raw/bid.csv` (layout below). Without them every notebook runs on
the synthetic series and says so in its first output.

The hashes are of the files as supplied (VN30: `VN_30_Historical_Data_Price_numeric_date_fixed.csv`;
BID: `Bank for Investment and Development Stock Price History.csv`). The provider, retrieval
date and terms of use are **not recorded in the files** and must be confirmed by the author
before any redistribution or publication that depends on them. Prices are as supplied; the
adjustment policy for BID (splits, dividends) is likewise undocumented.

## File layout accepted

`Date,Price,Open,High,Low,Vol.,Change %` with `month/day/year` dates, thousands separators and
`K/M/B` volume suffixes, **or** `ds,y[,open,high,low,volume]` with ISO dates. Rows are sorted
chronologically on load. Duplicate dates, non-positive or unparseable prices are rejected;
nothing is imputed.

## Known data issues (reported by notebook 01)

* **VN30 has four rows dated on a weekend** (2026-07-04, 2026-07-05, 2026-09-26, 2026-09-27)
  although the market is closed on weekends: a date artefact in the supplied file
  (the file name says the dates were "fixed"). Row order is used for all returns and
  horizons, so ordering is unaffected, but the calendar position of those sessions is
  uncertain. All of them fall in the test segment.
* One row per file has inconsistent open/high/low/close values. Such rows are masked in
  OHLC-derived features; the close is used as supplied.
* Some sessions are absent (gaps over five calendar days, counted in the quality table).
  Returns are between consecutive rows; the gap length is a feature.
* VN30 volume is missing before roughly 2012, and open = high = low = close for the earliest
  rows. The corresponding features are zero-filled with a `missing` flag.
