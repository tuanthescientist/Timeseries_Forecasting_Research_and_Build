# Attribution and reuse

Repository maintainer: [Tuan Tran](https://github.com/tuanthescientist).

This collection was curated from a local research workspace. Original notebook names
and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `source_manifest.json`; these identify source files,
not authorship or legal ownership. Existing markdown and comments in selected notebooks
are retained. Renaming and packaging do not establish originality.

The forecasting model families and their libraries are third-party work. Historical
experiments include TensorFlow, NeuralProphet, LightGBM, CatBoost, sktime/AutoARIMA,
and Amazon Chronos. Their own code, weights, and data remain subject to their respective
terms. This repository does not redistribute pretrained model weights.

Complete upstream provenance of the historical notebooks could not be established from
the local files. Before a blanket open-source license or academic originality claim is
made, identify borrowed notebooks, upstream URLs, authors, and required notices. No
blanket reuse license is granted in this initial release. Ask the maintainer about reuse.

When discussing experiments, reference the repository URL, exact Git commit, notebook
or benchmark entry point, data hash, and evaluation configuration. Do not cite the demo
as evidence of real-market performance.
